# ELE Practice Analytics — Outbound Kit

Peer-to-peer outreach to Nevada insurance-billing chiropractors. Everything here
leads with the **free denial review**, not a pitch. You are a DC talking to DCs.

---

## 1. Voice & rules (read once)

- **You are a chiropractor first.** The whole edge is "a DC who reads the data,
  not just the claim form." Say "I'm a DC here in Nevada" early and often.
- **Lead with the gift, not the ask.** Every first touch offers the free denial
  review. No demo, no call-to-buy in message one.
- **Short wins.** Cold emails under 120 words. Nobody at a front desk reads a wall.
- **One clear next step per message.** Reply "yes," book a slot, or send an export.
- **Specific beats clever.** "98940–43 coding errors" and "A/R past 75 days"
  signal you actually know the work. Generic "boost your revenue" reads as spam.
- **No fake endorsements.** Never imply the NV Board or the Nevada Chiropractic
  Association endorses you.
- **CAN-SPAM on every email:** real mailing address in the footer, a working
  opt-out line, no misleading subject lines. Honor opt-outs immediately.

**Cadence:** ~15–20 personalized touches/week. Sequence each contact:
Email 1 → (LinkedIn connect same week) → Email 2 (day 4–5) → Email 3 (day 9–11)
→ stop. High-value clinics also get the physical letter.

## 4. LinkedIn outreach

Connect the same week you email — a familiar name lifts email reply rates.

### Connection request (under 300 chars)

> Dr. [LastName] — fellow Nevada DC here. I moved from the table to the data side,
> helping insurance-billing practices recover denied claims. Would like to connect
> with local colleagues.

### First message (after they accept — do NOT pitch immediately)

> Thanks for connecting, Dr. [LastName]. Genuinely just building a network of
> Nevada DCs on the insurance-billing side.
>
> If it's ever useful: I run free denial reviews — send a billing export, I show
> you where claims are leaking. No strings. Happy to send details, or we can just
> stay connected. Either way, good to know you.

### If they engage

> Great — I'll send a one-page intake to your email. Takes ~5 min on your end and
> I turn the review around in about a week. What's the best address?

## 6. Objection responses (for replies & calls)

- **"We already have a biller."** — "Perfect — I'm not replacing them. I read the
  data your biller is too buried to mine, and hand them a ranked fix list. Think
  of it as a second set of eyes, free to start."
- **"We're too busy."** — "That's exactly the practices that leak the most. Your
  side is one export and ~5 minutes. I do the rest."
- **"Is this HIPAA-safe?"** — "Yes — BAA on request before anything moves, and I
  work off standard exports, not your live system."
- **"Do we have to switch systems?"** — "Never. I work on top of the EHR you
  already run."
- **"What does it cost after the free review?"** — "Nothing unless the numbers
  justify it. The review shows the dollars on the table; you decide from there."

`Replied?`, `Review sent?`, `Outcome`, `Opt-out?`.
